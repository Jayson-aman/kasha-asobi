## English (en)

- **App Name (≤30 chars)**
  Crinkle Play: Baby Sensory

- **Subtitle (≤30 chars)**
  Tap for sound, light & fun

- **Promotional Text (≤170 chars)**
  The crinkle of a plastic bag that soothes a fussy baby — now as a safe app. Tap for bursts of sound and light, calm with womb sounds, and play pretend shop. For ages 0–2.

- **Keywords (≤100 chars, comma-separated)**
  baby,infant,toddler,sensory,newborn,soothe,calm,white noise,womb sound,tap,pretend play,0-2,educational,kids,crinkle

- **Description**
  Babies love the crinkly “kasha-kasha” sound. This app synthesizes it live — tap the screen and sound, light, and shapes respond together in a multi-sensory play experience.

  ■ Why babies love it
  ・Resembles calming womb sounds
  ・The joy of cause & effect: “I touched it and it made a sound!”
  ・Multi-sensory: sound, light, and texture
  ・Imitating grown-ups (pretend play)

  ■ Features
  ・Tap or trace for crinkle sounds and bursts of light
  ・Womb-sound mode to soothe and settle
  ・Pretend shop play (bell, register, cake…)
  ・Child lock (press & hold to unlock) prevents mis-taps
  ・Screen-time timer / sleep mode
  ・Sound packs (animals, instruments, taiko) & dress-up themes
  ・Record a parent’s voice and play it back
  ・13+ languages

  ■ Pricing (unlock step by step)
  Free forever to play. For more, choose Standard, Pro, or Max.

  ※ Real plastic bags pose suffocation and choking risks. Never let a baby play with one directly — use a safe toy or this app, with an adult always present.

- **What's New**
  ・Added pretend-shop play mode
  ・Added child lock and screen-time timer
  ・Expanded multi-language support
